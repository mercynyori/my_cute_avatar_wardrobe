# my_cute_avatar_wardrobe
This is a avatar-digital wardrobe to play dress-up with my avatar. I used HTML/CSS and js to make designs  and code of the digital wardrobe and claude AI to help make things easier as i code and debugged.

A little digital dress-up game. Dress up a cute avatar, make her dance and play music!

Open it here: https://claude.ai/artifact/C88pLuRXKBQBx3fA1EbxjS

## How to use

1. Pick a tab at the bottom: Hats, Tops, Bottoms, Dresses, Shoes, Extras or My clothes.
2. Tap a piece to put it on the avatar. Tap it again to take it off.
3. Tap a colour dot to change the colour of the piece you picked.
4. Use the buttons at the top to make her play:
   - Wave 
   - Dance 
   - Jump  (you can also tap her)
   - Twirl 
   - Confetti 
5. Press Music for a music-box tune, or Your song  to play a song from your device.
6. Press Reset to take everything off.

## Add your own clothes

Go to the My clothes tab, press Add pictures and choose images from your folder.
PNG pictures with a transparent background look best.
Your pictures are only used in your browser, and you need to add them again each time you reopen the page.

## Good to know

This wardrobe enables music playing, changing clothes, adding new song changing color of the clothes and movement of the avatar.
I  used SVG markup for the avatar and browser page.
